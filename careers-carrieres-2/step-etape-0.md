<p>Understand each step in the process to become a police officer – from the application submission to cadet training.</p>
<nav class="gc-subway gc-subway-index">
  <h2>Steps</h2>
  <dl>
    <dt>
      <a href="#">Submit an online application</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>Start your application by creating an account on the Government of Canada's GC Jobs online portal.</dd>
    <dt>
      <a href="#">Online career presentation and entrance assessment</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>Watch a virtual presentation about the RCMP and complete an assessment to determine your cognitive ability.</dd>
    <dt>
      <a href="#">Submit the required forms and documents</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>Complete and submit forms through the Online Security Portal.</dd>
    <dt>
      <a href="#">Complete a suitability interview</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>Assess your suitability through the Regular Member Applicant Questionnaire and interview.</dd>
    <dt>
      <a href="#">Complete psychological and medical suitability assessments</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>Ensure you are medically cleared for frontline policing at the RCMP.</dd>
    <dt>
      <a href="#">Complete a field investigation and security assessment</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>Conclude your application with a background check to obtain your security clearance.</dd>
  </dl>
</nav>
