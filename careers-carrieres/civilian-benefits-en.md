<p>Work with a diverse and respected organization that offers continuous career development and benefits designed to support both your professional growth and overall well‑being.</p>
<p>As an employee, you’ll enjoy a range of benefits:</p>
<dl class="dl-horizontal">
    <dt>Opportunities across Canada</dt>
    <dd>
    <ul>
        <li>a wide range of roles available nationwide</li>
        <li>opportunities to acquire new skills and expand your knowledge</li>
    </ul>
    </dd>
    <dt>Health and financial benefits</dt>
    <dd>
    <ul>
        <li>comprehensive <a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/topics/benefit-plans/plans.html">medical and dental coverage</a></li>
        <li><a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/topics/benefit-plans/plans.html">pension plan</a> to support long‑term financial security</li>
    </ul>
    </dd>
    <dt>Leave and work‑life balance</dt>
    <dd>
    <ul>
        <li>vacation leave</li>
        <li>sick leave</li>
        <li>family‑related leave</li>
        <li>other leave options to support personal needs</li>
    </ul>
    </dd>
    <dt>Continuous learning and career advancement</dt>
    <dd>
    <ul>
        <li>access to numerous internal training courses</li>
        <li>on‑the‑job learning through coaching and mentoring</li>
        <li>opportunities to attend conferences and workshops</li>
    </ul>
    </dd>
    <dt>Inclusive and accessible workplace</dt>
    <dd>
    <ul>
        <li>a strong commitment to employment equity</li>
        <li>a workplace that reflects the diversity of Canadian society</li>
        <li>safe, respectful, and barrier‑free work environments</li>
    </ul>
    </dd>
</dl>
