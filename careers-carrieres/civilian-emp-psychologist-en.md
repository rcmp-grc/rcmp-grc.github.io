<section id="s1">
  <h2>Lifeline to a healthy workplace</h2>
  <p>
    Psychologists play an important role in the RCMP. They deliver services that help keep our officers safe and healthy. Psychologists protect RCMP officers from the impacts of psychological risks by:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>identifying and evaluating mental health issues as early as possible</li>
    <li>helping those with psychological health problems return to their best health</li>
    <li>building a trusting relationship with our officers so that they are comfortable seeking mental health support when needed</li>
  </ul>
</section>
<section id="s2">
  <h2>What the work involves</h2>
  <p>You will perform psychological assessments of RCMP officers. This includes but is not limited to:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>reviewing medical files</li>
    <li>conducting clinical interviews, including administering recognized psychological tests</li>
    <li>identifying areas that need intervention</li>
    <li>educating officers on psychological health</li>
    <li>making recommendations and referrals</li>
  </ul>
  <p>You will also have access to regular and timely peer meetings for case discussions and consultation.</p>
</section>
<section id="s3">
  <h2>Top reasons to work for the RCMP</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>37.5&#160;hour work week (overtime may be required in some circumstances)</li>
    <li>enviable indexed pension plan</li>
    <li>medical and dental health care benefits</li>
    <li>four-week's paid vacation within a year of being hired</li>
    <li>training opportunities</li>
    <li>reimbursement of licensing and relevant association fees</li>
    <li>no office overhead</li>
    <li>competitive pay ($95,233 to $112,957) with terminable allowances:
      <ul>
        <li>master's level registered psychologists – $6,000</li>
        <li>doctoral level registered psychologists – $12,000</li>
      </ul>
    </li>
  </ul>
  <p>If you want to make a real difference in the lives of others, and help keep Canadians safe – this is the career for you!</p>
</section>
