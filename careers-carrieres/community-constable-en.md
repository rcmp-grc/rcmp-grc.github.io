<section id="s1">
  <h2>Who we are</h2>
  <p>We are armed, uniformed peace officers who serve in our home communities. Community constables are well positioned for the role because we're familiar with our community's language, culture and geography.</p>
</section>

<section id="s2">
  <h2>What we do</h2>
  <p>Our main mandate is to engage our communities in crime prevention and reduction activities.</p>
  <p>We can also support the work of police officers in their daily duties if required.</p>
</section>

<section id="s3">
  <h2>Requirements and training</h2>
  <p>As a community constable, you must meet standards similar to those for regular members. We spend 23&#160;weeks training at the RCMP Academy (Depot) in Regina, Saskatchewan.</p>
</section>

</section>
