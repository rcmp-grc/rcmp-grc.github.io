<section id="s1">
    <p>You will need to get medically cleared by your physician. Your physician will need to fill out the following form:</p>
    <ul class="gc-link-list" role="list">
        <li>
          <a href="6627e.pdf" class="gc-pdf-link" download>
            <i class="fa-solid fa-file-arrow-down" aria-hidden="true"></i>
            <span>
              <span class="gc-link-title">Physical Employment Standard (PES) (RCMP&#160;6627)</span>
              <span class="gc-link-meta" data-pdf-meta>PDF</span>
            </span>
          </a>
        </li>
    </ul>
    <p>Then you can schedule the police fitness assessment with your EPO coordinator.</p>
    <p>You will need to successfully complete the RCMP's police fitness assessment (PFA), field standard; which is performed in the general duty police uniform or the equivalent. The field version includes a 500-meter run, a lift/carry/run evacuation task and a medicine ball throw for distance.</p>
    <p>You will need to wear:</p>
    <ul>
        <li>general duty police uniform. If you don't have one, wear a t-shirt and pants that can comfortably accommodate a duty belt or weighted belt</li>
        <li>soft body armour vest and duty belt. If you don't have this apparel, we will provide them to you</li>
        <li>running shoes or duty boots, based on your preference</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Watch the <a href="#">RCMP Police Fitness Assessment video</a>.</p>
</section>
