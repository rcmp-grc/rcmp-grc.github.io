<p>In the Experienced Police Officer application process, you must follow the steps below, which may differ based on province or territory.</p>

<nav class="gc-subway gc-subway-index">
  <h2>Steps in the process</h2>
  <dl>
    <dt>
      <a href="epo-application-candidature-pe-1-en.html">Express interest</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>Contact an Experienced Police Officer Coordinator to discuss available postings and submit the application form and questionnaire to start the application.</dd>
    <dt>
      <a href="epo-application-candidature-pe-2-en.html">Submit forms</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>Complete and submit forms and documents including a cover letter and resume.</dd>
    <dt>
      <a href="epo-application-candidature-pe-3-en.html">Pass the Police Fitness Assessment</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>Assess your physical abilities by successfully completing the RCMP Police Fitness Assessment.</dd>
    <dt>
      <a href="epo-application-candidature-pe-4-en.html">Complete a suitability interview</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>Get an assessment on core competencies, core values, and traits required of an RCMP police officer.</dd>
    <dt>
      <a href="epo-application-candidature-pe-5-en.html">Undergo medical and psychological assessments</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>See an RCMP-designated physician and psychologist for a medical and psychological suitability assessment.</dd>
    <dt>
      <a href="epo-application-candidature-pe-6-en.html">Undergo a field investigation and security screening</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>The RCMP will conduct a thorough investigation into your background to help assess your suitability.</dd>
    <dt>
      <a href="epo-application-candidature-pe-7-en.html">Complete the 3-week training</a>
    </dt>
    <dd>Conclude your process with tailored training to prepare for your first posting.</dd>
  </dl>
</nav>
