<p>Federal Policing offers unique opportunities for experienced law enforcement professionals to contribute to some of Canada's most complex and impactful investigations. Whether you are currently serving with the RCMP or with a municipal, provincial, territorial, or Indigenous police service, there are pathways to build a career in Federal Policing.</p> 
<section id="s1">
    <h2>Federal Policing at the RCMP</h2>
    <p>Federal Policing protects Canada's safety, security, and economic interests by investigating complex criminal threats and collaborating with partners across Canada and internationally.</p>
    <section id="s1-1">
        <h3>Potential Federal Policing opportunities</h3>
        <ul>
            <li>Serious and Organized Crime </li>
            <li>National Security </li>
            <li>Financial Crime </li>
            <li>Border Integrity </li>
            <li>Protective Policing </li>
            <li>Intelligence Operations</li>
        </ul>
    </section>
</section>
<section id="s2">
    <h2>Pathways to Federal Policing</h2> 
    <section id="s2-1">
        <h3>Experienced Police Officer Program</h3>
        <p>The Experienced Police Officer (EPO) Program provides a pathway for qualified police officers from municipal, provincial, territorial, and Indigenous police services to join the RCMP and contribute to Federal Policing operations. Experienced officers bring valuable investigative expertise, operational knowledge, and leadership experience that can support some of Canada's most complex criminal investigations.</p>
    </section>
    <section id="s2-2">
        <h3>RCMP regular member</h3>  
        <p>Many Federal Policing employees begin their careers as RCMP regular members. This pathway allows officers to develop frontline policing experience and investigative skills before pursuing specialized opportunities within Federal Policing.</p>
        <p>The typical career pathway begins with Cadet training, progresses to general duty policing, followed by the development of specialized experience, and can ultimately lead to opportunities in Federal Policing.</p>
        <p>Federal Policing positions are most often staffed by experienced RCMP members who have developed strong investigative and operational expertise throughout their careers.</p>
    </section>
</section>
<section id="s3">
    <h2>How to apply</h2>
    <p><b>Experienced police officers</b> can learn more about eligibility requirements, required policing experience, and the application process through the RCMP <a href="/en/careers-rcmp/experienced-police-officers">Experienced Police Officer Program</a>.</p>   
    <p><b>Current RCMP regular members</b> interested in a career in Federal Policing are encouraged to discuss their career goals with their supervisors and career development advisors, and to identify Federal Policing as an area of interest as they explore future career opportunities.</p>
    <p>Learn about eligibility requirements, the recruitment process, and what to expect during training to become an RCMP <a href="/en/careers-rcmp/police-officer-careers">Police officer</a>.</p>
</section>
