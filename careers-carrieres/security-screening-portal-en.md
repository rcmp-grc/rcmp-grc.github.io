<section id="s1">
  <h2>Sign into the Security Screening Portal</h2>
  <p>The Security Screening Portal is available for individuals to securely submit information to the RCMP.</p>

  <section class="alert alert-info">
    <h2>Note</h2>
    <p>To access the portal, you must register for a GCKey or with a sign-in partner.</p>
  </section>
  <p><a href="https://www.services.rcmp-grc.gc.ca/chooser-eng.html" class="rc-btn rc-btn--primary" type="submit">Sign in to the Security Screening Portal</a></p>
</section>

<section id="s2">
  <h2>Collection of personal information </h2>
  <p>Upon obtaining informed consent, the screening agency can collect, create, use, disclose, retain and dispose of personal information using Treasury Board of Canada Secretariat prescribed forms and models for the purpose of security screening in accordance with the <cite>Privacy Act</cite> and the Directive on Privacy Practices. </p>
  <p>For more information, consult Appendix B: Mandatory Procedures for Management of Personal Information for the Purpose of Security Screening in the <a href="https://www.tbs-sct.canada.ca/pol/(S(5xos1g45ufdaaz45lxatyqms))/doc-eng.aspx?id=32805&section=html">Directive on Security Screening</a>. </p>
</section>

<section id="s3">
  <h2>Security screening process</h2>
  <dl>
    <dt>1. Access the portal </dt>
    <dd>You will receive an invitation email containing all information and instructions for the portal, including how to register for the first time. </dd>
    <dt>2. Submit security information </dt>
    <dd>After providing informed consent, complete the security screening forms and submit all requested information. </dd>
    <dt>3. Screening review </dt>
    <dd>The security screening team will complete the security review. You may be contacted for clarification or supporting information. </dd>
    <dt>4. Completion of screening </dt>
    <dd>Once your security review has been completed, your results will be provided to the appropriate personnel. Any further communication will be received from your hiring representative. </dd>
  </dl>
</section>

<section id="s4">
  <h2>PDF forms</h2>
  <p>The RCMP produces forms in Portable Document Format (PDF). You can complete and save forms using the free <a href="https://get.adobe.com/reader/">Adobe Reader</a>: </p>
  <ol>
    <li>download and save the PDF form to your computer </li>
    <li>launch Adobe Reader </li>
    <li>open the file from within Adobe Reader; you can now complete and save your PDF form </li>
  </ol>

  <section class="alert alert-info">
    <h3 class="h4">Note</h3>
    <p>RCMP fillable or savable PDF forms have been tested for compatibility with Adobe Reader 9 and higher and may not be fully supported in other PDF viewers and on some mobile devices. </p>
  </section>
</section>

<section id="s5">
  <h2>Troubleshooting and support </h2>
  <p>If you encounter issues with the applicant portal, we recommend the following troubleshooting steps to resolve: </p>
  <ul>
    <li>clear your browsing data and cache. Once done, close down your browser and re-open a new window </li>
    <li>ensure your GCKey is active and/or can successfully sign in using sign-in partners services </li>
  </ul>
  <p>If you have any questions, or require additional support with the applicant portal, please reach out to your regional Departmental Security Screening unit by clicking on your province/territory below. Please include your full name and applicant ID within the email. </p>
