<details class="mrgn-bttm-lg">
  <summary><span class="h4" style="display:inline;">Browser and system requirements</span></summary>
  <p>You may be able to use Canadian Firearms Program (CFP) online services with different web browsers. However, some browsers may not be fully tested with these services.</p>
  <p>If you experience technical issues, try using a different browser.</p>
  <p>To access CFP services, your browser must:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>support Transport Layer Security (TLS) 1.2 or higher</li>
    <li>have TLS 1.2 or higher enabled</li>
    <li>accept cookies</li>
    <li>have JavaScript enabled</li>
  </ul>
  <p>TLS 1.3 is recommended.</p>
  <p>Devices that are more than 5 years old may not work properly with CFP online services.</p>
  <p>For the best experience, use a modern desktop or laptop computer.</p>
  <p>Technical support is not available for mobile devices, including:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>smartphones</li>
    <li>tablets</li>
    <li>gaming systems</li>
  </ul>
  <p>This includes devices running Android or Apple iOS.</p>
  <p class="h5">Technical support notice</p>
  <p>The CFP is not responsible for issues related to downloading or installing software.</p>
  <p>For technical support, contact the software provider directly.</p>
</details>
<section aria-labelledby="qa-heading" class="well brdr-0 brdr-rds-0">
  <h2 class="mrgn-tp-0" id="qa-heading">Questions and answers</h2>
  <div class="row">
    <div class="col-sm-6">
      <section aria-labelledby="qa-app-status-heading">
        <h3 id="qa-app-status-heading">Checking your application status</h3>
        <details>
          <summary>My application status does not include enough detail. What should I do?</summary>
          <p>The online status tool provides general updates only. It will usually show whether your application:</p>
          <ul>
            <li>has been received</li>
            <li>is being processed</li>
            <li>has been completed</li>
          </ul>
          <p>If you submitted your application by mail, allow 2 to 4 weeks for it to appear in the system.</p>
          <p>For more detailed information, contact the Canadian Firearms Program at <a href="tel:18007314000" class="text-nowrap">1-800-731-4000</a>.</p>
        </details>
      </section>
      <section aria-labelledby="qa-register-heading">
        <h3 id="qa-register-heading">Registering firearms</h3>
        <details>
          <summary>Is my firearm registered immediately after I submit the application online?</summary>
          <p>No.</p>
          <p>Submitting your application online does not complete the registration process. Your application must still go through several validation steps to confirm that your licence and firearm privileges remain valid.</p>
          <p>After submitting your application, you will receive a confirmation number showing that your registration request is being processed.</p>
          <p>You will not be able to print a registration certificate immediately.</p>
          <p>The Canadian Firearms Program may contact you if additional information is required.</p>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary>I need to register a firearm but I do not have Internet access. What are my options?</summary>
          <p>Public Internet access may be available at:</p>
          <ul>
            <li>public libraries</li>
            <li>Internet cafés</li>
            <li>community access locations</li>
          </ul>
          <p>You are not required to use the online service.</p>
          <p>You can also:</p>
          <ul>
            <li>download a paper application form from the website</li>
            <li>request an application package by mail through the Canadian Firearms Program</li>
          </ul>
        </details>
      </section>
      <section aria-labelledby="qa-signup-heading">
        <h3 id="qa-signup-heading">Signing up for online services</h3>
        <details>
          <summary>Why do I need to provide an email address?</summary>
          <p>The Canadian Firearms Program is moving from paper mail to electronic communication where possible.</p>
          <p>Using email:</p>
          <ul>
            <li>reduces costs</li>
            <li>improves service delivery</li>
            <li>allows faster communication with clients</li>
          </ul>
          <p>Email updates may include important information about your firearms licence and related services.</p>
          <p>If you do not have an email account, you can create one using a free email service provider.</p>
        </details>
      </section>
    </div>
    <div class="col-sm-6">
      <section aria-labelledby="qa-renew-heading">
        <h3 id="qa-renew-heading">Renewing your licence</h3>
        <details>
          <summary>Can businesses renew firearms licences online?</summary>
          <p>No.</p>
          <p>Business firearms licences cannot currently be renewed online.</p>
        </details>
        <details>
          <summary>Is my licence renewed as soon as I submit the application online?</summary>
          <p>No.</p>
          <p>Submitting your renewal application online does not automatically renew your licence. Your application must still be reviewed and validated.</p>
          <p>After you apply, you will receive a confirmation number showing that your renewal request is being processed.</p>
          <p>You may be contacted if more information is needed.</p>
        </details>
      </section>
      <section aria-labelledby="qa-print-heading">
        <h3 id="qa-print-heading">Printing documents</h3>
        <details>
          <summary>I cannot print a document. What should I do?</summary>
          <p>If you can save and open the document but cannot print it, there may be an issue with your printer or connection.</p>
          <p>Check that:</p>
          <ul>
            <li>your printer cables are connected properly</li>
            <li>your wireless network is working correctly</li>
            <li>your printer is turned on and connected</li>
          </ul>
          <p>If the problem continues:</p>
          <ul>
            <li>review your printer manual</li>
            <li>contact technical support for your printer or device manufacturer</li>
          </ul>
        </details>
      </section>
