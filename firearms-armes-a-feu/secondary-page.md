<p>If you are not a Canadian resident, you are subject to several additional rules and requirements about firearms beyond those that apply to Canadian residents.</p>
</section>

<section id="s1">
<h2>Visiting Canada with a firearm</h2>
<p>If you plan to bring a firearm with you when you visit Canada, you must be at least 18 years old and you must complete <a href="/en/firearms/firearms-forms-and-reports#s1"><abbr>RCMP</abbr> Form 5589: Non-Resident Firearms Declaration Form</a> before you arrive at your entry point. Do not sign the form ahead of time. This is because a Canadian Border Services Agency customs officer must witness your signature. The fee to submit the form is $25.</p>

<p>Once it is signed, this form serves as a temporary (60-day) licence that applies only to you and only to the firearm(s) listed on  it. You can renew it at no cost by <a href="/en/firearms/contact-chief-firearms-officer">contacting the chief firearms officer</a> of the province or territory you are visiting before it expires.</p>

<p>The declaration form covers up to 2 firearms. If you plan to bring more than 2 firearms into Canada, you must complete <a href="/en/firearms/firearms-forms-and-reports#s1"><abbr>RCMP</abbr> form 5590: Non-Resident Firearm Declaration Continuation Sheet</a>, listing all the firearms you are bringing.</p>

<p>The declaration form also permits you to buy ammunition in Canada for the firearm(s) listed on it.</p>

<p>If any of the firearms you are bringing into Canada are restricted, you must <a href="/en/firearms/contact-chief-firearms-officer">contact the chief firearms officer</a> of your entry point in advance to apply for an <a href="/en/firearms/firearms-safety-training-transport-and-storage/authorization-transport#s2">authorization to transport restricted firearms</a>.</p>

<p>If you visit Canada frequently or for long periods of time, we encourage you to <a href="/en/firearms/apply-firearms-licence">apply for a possession and acquisition licence</a>. Once  you have that licence, you will no longer need to complete a declaration form every time you enter Canada, and there will be no additional fees from bringing your firearms into Canada. However, you will still need to declare all firearms when you enter the country. Also, you must be prepared to show your licence and the registration certificates for any restricted firearms to a Canadian Border Services Agency customs officer. </p>
</section>

<section id="s2">
<h2>Borrowing firearms in Canada</h2>
<p>You do not need a licence to borrow a firearm in Canada, as long as you will be under the direct and immediate supervision of an adult with a valid licence for that class of firearm. If you do not have appropriate supervision, you will need either a possession and acquisition licence or a non-resident temporary borrowing licence for non-restricted firearms. The temporary borrowing licence is valid for 60 days. The fee is $30.</p>

<p>You can apply for a t<a href="/en/firearms/apply-firearms-licence">emporary borrowing licence online</a> or by mail by completing <abbr><a href="/en/firearms/firearms-forms-and-reports#s1">RCMP</a></abbr><a href="/en/firearms/firearms-forms-and-reports#s1"> form 5513: Application for a Non-resident Temporary Borrowing Licence for Non-restricted Firearms</a>. </p>

<p>To apply for a temporary borrowing licence, you will need:</p>
<ul>
  <li>a valid email address</li>
  <li>2 pieces of government-issued identification</li>
  <li>a credit card or Visa or MasterCard debit card</li>
</ul>
<p>You will also need a sponsor associated with your intended activity, such as an outfitter or Canadian resident licensed to hunt in the province or territory where you will be hunting. The sponsor must have a valid email address.</p>
<p>Your temporary borrowing licence permits:</p>
<ul>
  <li>hunting under the supervision of an outfitter or other person authorized to organize hunting services in Canada</li>
  <li>hunting with a Canadian resident who has the proper firearms and hunting licences</li>
  <li>participating in a shooting competition</li>
  <li>target shooting at an approved shooting club or range</li>
  <li>taking part in a historical re-enactment or display</li>
  <li>engaging in a business or scientific activity in a  remote area where you need firearms to control animal predators</li>
  <li>taking part in a parade, pageant or similar event</li>
  <li>using firearms for movie, television, video or theatrical productions, or publishing activities</li>
</ul>
	<section class="alert alert-info">
	<h3>Note</h3>
	<p>A minor may <a href="/en/firearms/licensing/minors">apply for a minor's licence</a>, which will allow them to borrow a non-restricted rifle or shotgun for purposes such as hunting with a properly licensed outfitter.</p>
	</section>

<h2>Moving to Canada</h2>
<p>If you plan to move to Canada with any firearms, you must have a possession and acquisition licence.</p>
<p>Learn more about <a href="/en/firearms/apply-firearms-licence">applying for a possession and acquisition licence</a>.</p>
