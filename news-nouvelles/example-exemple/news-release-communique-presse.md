# News Release

## RCMP introduces Bob the Polar Bear as unofficial northern community mascot

**From:** National Communications and Public Affairs/
**Date:** May 14, 2026/
**Ottawa, Ontario**

The Royal Canadian Mounted Police is pleased to introduce Bob the Polar Bear as its unofficial northern community mascot in support of outreach, youth engagement, and winter safety awareness activities across Canada.

Bob will participate in selected RCMP community events, school visits, public safety campaigns, and northern engagement initiatives throughout the year.

The initiative was developed to support positive and approachable conversations with communities, particularly youth and families, while promoting important public safety messages in a creative and accessible way.

“Community engagement works best when people feel comfortable participating,” said Chief Superintendent Marie Delaney, National Communications and Public Affairs. “Bob helps create that environment. He brings curiosity, humour, and a surprising level of enthusiasm for emergency preparedness.”

Bob first appeared during a winter safety event in Nunavut earlier this year and quickly became popular with local families, volunteers, and RCMP employees. Since then, requests for appearances have continued to grow.

During outreach activities, Bob helps promote:

- winter travel safety;
- emergency preparedness;
- youth engagement;
- anti-bullying initiatives;
- environmental awareness; and
- community partnerships.

Although Bob is known for his calm personality and oversized RCMP scarf, organizers note there have been several unofficial attempts by him to inspect snack tables before events begin.

“Bob reminds us that building relationships can happen in simple ways,” said Staff Sergeant Claire Bouchard, Community Outreach Services. “A conversation at a community event can leave a lasting impression, especially for young people.”

As part of the initiative, the RCMP will continue working with local organizations, schools, Indigenous communities, municipalities, and emergency management partners to support safe and inclusive public events across the country.

Upcoming appearances for Bob are expected to include:

- winter festivals;
- school safety presentations;
- family outreach events;
- northern recruitment activities; and
- community wellness campaigns.

The RCMP confirms that Bob does not hold peace officer status and should not be approached with parking complaints, traffic disputes, or requests to investigate missing fish.

## Quick facts

- Bob is a fictional RCMP outreach mascot inspired by northern wildlife.
- Bob’s first public appearance took place during a winter community event in Nunavut.
- The mascot supports public education and community engagement activities.
- Bob’s favourite activity is reported to be handing out winter safety checklists, although witnesses say he mainly enjoys the stickers.
