<p>Conduct hearings are open to the public. A list of all of the currently scheduled conduct hearings can be found below.</p>
<section class="alert alert-info">
  <h2 class="h3">Note</h2>
  <p>Hearing dates, times and locations are subject to change. The online hearing schedule is updated as information becomes available.</p>
</section>
<p>For media inquires, regarding conduct hearings, please contact the provincial (Divisional) <a href="https://rcmp.ca/en/news/rcmp-media-relations-offices">media relations office</a>.</p>
<p>Available written conduct board decisions are <a href="https://decisions.rcmp.gc.ca/rcmp/en/nav.do">published online</a>.</p>
<p>If you would like to obtain a copy of an Adjudications Board decision issued pursuant to the previous <cite><abbr>RCMP</abbr> Act</cite> (prior to November 28, 2014), please send an email to: <a href="mailto:RCMP.AdjudicationRegistrar-GreffierArbitrage.GRC@rcmp-grc.gc.ca">rcmp.adjudicationregistrar-greffierarbitrage.grc@rcmp-grc.gc.ca</a>.</p>
